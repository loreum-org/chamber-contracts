# Koeppelmann on Bermuda — Safe-native privacy / private multisig

Primary social capture for Chamber’s Safe-adjacent competitive corpus. Quotes
below are verbatim from the linked posts (accessed 2026-09-11). This file does
not assert Chamber product decisions.

## Speaker context (not a product claim)

Martin Köppelmann (@koeppelmann) is a **co-founder of Gnosis** (public bio:
Gnosis co-founder / CEO). Safe originated as a Gnosis spin-off; Köppelmann
remains a public voice around the Safe / Gnosis ecosystem. Treat that as
**author identity**, not as evidence that Bermuda is Safe-official or that
Chamber should change roadmap.

  a Bermuda founder or issuer.
- The product claim in his post is **Safe-controlled privacy-pool funds**
  (access/recovery of the Safe ⇒ access to those funds) and “first privacy
  solution that works fully with Safe.”
- Bermuda’s own announcement names **private multisig**, **private
  transactions**, **payroll**, and **other DeFi use cases** on EVM.
